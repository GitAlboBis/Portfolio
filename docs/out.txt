model granular materials with friction and cohesion. Position-
based dynamics traditionally models friction by damping veloc-
ity after the position-based constraint solve has completed [Mu¨ller
et al. 2007]. This method cannot model static friction because the
frictional damping forces cannot correct for the position changes
already made during the constraint solve. Consequently, particle
positions drift, and piles quickly collapse. We address this problem
using a novel formulation that applies friction at the position level.
