## 0. What is claimed in print (cases.json)

Source: `public/proto/rivista/parts/cases.json`, entries `sersan-io` (tile 04) and `domus-tua` (tile 05). Every number below is already printed on the portfolio with a `src` pointer into `PRODUCT.md`; nothing else about these two sites may be printed without the same backing.

**sersan-io** (role: sole engineer and designer; period Jun – Sep 2026; public at sersan.io)
- Lead: "The studio's WebGPU-first site: one continuous 3D canvas carrying seventeen scroll-locked scenes, built alone."
- Stack as printed: Next.js 16 · React 19 · three.js (WebGPU/TSL) · React Three Fiber · GSAP + Lenis · Tailwind v4 · Vercel.
- Numbers: **17** scroll-locked scenes on one canvas · **48,000** particles in the wordmark intro, "computed on the GPU, beside a particle brand mark" · **80,000** cells in the portrait morph, "lit from generated depth maps" · Lighthouse Accessibility **100** and SEO **100**.
- Also claimed: "an automatic fallback for machines without WebGPU and recovery if the graphics context is lost"; "a custom refractive crystal material"; "a selective-bloom post-processing chain"; "a reduced-motion path for every 3D section" and "a non-JavaScript equivalent for every 3D section"; "a device-tiering system runs the full scene on weak mobile GPUs at a quarter of the pixels, without removing a single effect"; the mobile home page "cut to two-thirds of its length".
- Three captured frames (October 2026, WebGPU): `sersan-io-wordmark` (S, E, R drawn in white particles, the rest a cyan cloud arriving from the right), `sersan-io-blackhole` ("raymarched under the wordmark, with the hero at rest": dark sphere, thin bright ring, bent blue streams, sparse starfield), `sersan-io-portrait` (Alberto's own portrait "drawn in cells, inside an orbit of labels"; the telemetry columns behind it are captioned as decoration, not readings). The portrait caption deliberately does not name the sersan.io section it sits in: he is the studio's Creative Lead, not its founder.

**domus-tua** (role: art direction and build; client work; URL pending)
- Lead: "Real-estate brand platform with a cinematic scroll experience, and mobile LCP cut from 4.75 s to 1.74 s on real phones."
- What is claimed: "animated intro, arched page transitions, chapter-based storytelling" with complete reduced-motion and no-JavaScript alternatives and no layout shift; mobile blocking time 572 ms → 274 ms; the delay "traced to the cookie banner and the intro rebuilt to run in CSS rather than JavaScript".
- Stack as printed: Next.js 16 · GSAP · Lenis · Supabase. No WebGL is claimed for Domus Tua.
- The one capture shown is the method section (ivory and red, cards along a winding red line); the home hero was withdrawn because it shows the client founder's face and name.
