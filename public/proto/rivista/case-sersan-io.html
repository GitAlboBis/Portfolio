01 What it is

One canvas,seventeen scenes

I rebuilt the studio's public site single-handedly, as engineer and designer, into a WebGPU-first experience: one continuous 3D canvas carries seventeen scroll-locked scenes across the site, with an automatic fallback for machines without WebGPU and recovery if the graphics context is lost.

A device-tiering system runs the full scene on weak mobile GPUs at a quarter of the pixels, without removing a single effect. The mobile layouts were rebuilt, accessible touch targets restored after tracing every undersized control to a single styling rule, and the mobile home page cut to two-thirds of its length.
